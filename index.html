STKFormer: Data Sources

Data access guide for long-term PM2.5 forecasting with industrial influence and regional spatial context.

STKFormer combines air-quality observations, meteorological variables, industrial spatial information, and historical aerosol optical depth (AOD) to support multi-source spatiotemporal forecasting.

Study period2021–2023
Study citiesBeijing · Shanghai · Guangzhou · Tangshan
Monitoring network78 air-quality stations
Forecast horizon72 hours
